FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any design experience to use Aurora?

Not at all. Aurora is built so anyone on your team — PM, founder, or engineer — can describe what they need in plain language and get a usable design out. Designers can go further with fine-grained manual controls.

Can I export the code Aurora generates?

Yes. Every screen can be exported as clean, framework-ready markup, or handed to engineering as a structured spec with tokens and component references intact.

Does Aurora work with our existing design system?

Aurora can ingest your tokens, components, and spacing rules, then apply them automatically to everything it generates — so new screens stay on-brand from the first draft.

Is there a limit on team size?

No. Pro and Enterprise plans support unlimited seats and workspaces — pricing is based on usage, not headcount.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your billing settings — no long-term contracts required.
