About Val

Quilting the Hooper community trusts

Val has quilted dozens of customers' quilt tops into finished, ready-to-love heirlooms. Customers describe her work as beautiful and impeccable, her prices as fair, and Val herself as professional, easy to work with, and genuinely invested in getting every detail right.

Whether it's your first quilt or your twentieth, she'll work with you to find a custom design that fits your fabric, your story, and your budget.

Services

What Val offers

Every quilt is different — call or stop by to talk through what yours needs.

Custom machine quilting

A design chosen and stitched to fit your quilt top — from classic patterns to something entirely custom.

Edge-to-edge patterns

A consistent all-over design stitched corner to corner — a popular, budget-friendly finish.

Design consultation

Not sure what design suits your fabric? Val will help you pick a pattern and thread that brings it together.

Personal & heirloom quilts

From baby quilts to family heirlooms, every project gets the same careful attention to detail.
